Privacy Policy for Mochy Backup

Last updated: November 25, 2026

1. Data We Access

When you sign in with Google in Mochy Backup, the app requests access only to the backup files it creates in your Google Drive and basic account identification (your display name/email shown inside the backup settings screen).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your Google Drive access is used solely to upload, list, download, and restore your personal Mochy Backup archives directly between your Android device and your Google Drive account.

3. Data Storage and Sharing

Mochy Backup does not operate any developer-owned backend server. OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device in Android's private app storage, and backup files are stored exclusively in your own Google Drive. We never sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties or use it to train AI/ML models.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can sign out inside the app at any time to wipe local tokens, delete your backup files directly from Google Drive, and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Mochy Backup's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data inquiries, open an issue at github.com/Rizky088/Mochy-Music-Custom/issues.
